# Support

ShadeRoute is a public working prototype with no service-level, response-time or
incident-response commitment.

## Public support

Use [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/MasteraSnackin/shade-route/issues/new/choose)
for reproducible bugs, data/model discrepancies and scoped feature requests.
Search existing issues first and use the form that matches the request.

Provide the application commit or version, pilot area, browser/device, expected
result and observed result. For time-dependent shade problems, include the local
London date and time shown by the application. Remove names, home/work addresses,
precise journey history and other personal information.

## Not a support channel

- Do not use GitHub Issues for vulnerabilities; follow
  [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
- Do not ask maintainers to make an urgent journey, medical, workplace or
  emergency decision.
- Do not publish API keys, provider responses containing personal information or
  unreviewed field-observation exports.

ShadeRoute does not provide medical advice, emergency coordination or a route
safety guarantee. For an immediate medical or public-safety emergency, contact
the appropriate emergency service. For workplace heat risk, follow the relevant
employer, regulator and on-site procedures.
