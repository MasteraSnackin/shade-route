# Code of conduct

## Our standard

Contributors and maintainers must make participation respectful, accessible and
free from harassment. Constructive disagreement about evidence, safety and
design is welcome; personal attacks are not.

Expected behaviour includes:

- using clear, inclusive language and respecting different lived experience;
- challenging claims with evidence and stating uncertainty plainly;
- handling location, health, employment and field-observation information with
  care;
- accepting correction and keeping review focused on the work; and
- avoiding pressure to weaken safety, privacy, accessibility or attribution.

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualised
attention, doxxing, publishing private information, deliberate misinformation
and sustained disruption of project spaces.

## Scope and enforcement

This standard applies in repository discussions, pull requests, issues and
other spaces where someone represents ShadeRoute.

Do not publish sensitive incident details. Ask the repository owner for a
private reporting channel through the minimal process in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). The owner may edit or remove content and temporarily
or permanently restrict participation when behaviour threatens people or the
project. No independent appeals contact or response-time commitment currently
exists; that governance gap must be resolved before a wider public pilot.
